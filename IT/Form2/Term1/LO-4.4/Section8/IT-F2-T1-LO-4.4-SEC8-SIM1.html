How to Play Social Feed Sleuth

Welcome, Investigator! You are reviewing the TriniConnect school social feed to stop misinformation and promote digital ethics.

1. Scroll to Discover New Posts (Facebook-Style Loading)
As you scroll down the feed, top animated loading banners and skeleton shimmers will simulate live posts arriving in real time.
2. Click "🔍 Inspect Source"
Examine the embedded 4 clues: Author (credentials), Date (recency), Purpose (educational vs. selling ads), and Evidence (citations & uncropped photos).
3. Make Your Classification Decision
Choose 🟢 Responsible Sharing for verified facts, cited archives, ethical consent, and helpful corrections. Choose 🔴 Inappropriate Use for unverified claims, clickbait ads, unconsented private photos, out-of-context cropped photos, or edited images.
