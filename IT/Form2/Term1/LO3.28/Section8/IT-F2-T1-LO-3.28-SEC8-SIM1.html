How to Play The Distortion Lab

Welcome to the Trinidad and Tobago Secondary School Digital Media Art Gallery! Your mission is to prepare 5 photos for our annual exhibition.

  • 🔵 Corner Handles (Proportional Scaling): Drag any of the 4 corner handles to scale the image while locking its original aspect ratio. Pictures stay natural and circular objects stay round.
  • 🟠 Side & Top Handles (Non-Proportional Distortion): Dragging side or top handles stretches or squashes the image, breaking its aspect ratio and making it look like a funny rubbery disaster!
  • 📈 Distortion Meter: Watch the live meter. Keep the needle in the 🟢 Green zone (0% distortion). If it enters 🔴 Red, undo and resize using corner handles.
  • ↺ Ctrl+Z Undo: Press Ctrl+Z or click Undo at any time to instantly reverse distortion mistakes.
  • 🔒 Mount in Exhibition: Once the image fits the golden frame with 0% distortion, click Mount in Exhibition to lock in your score!
