How to Play Review Rescue

Welcome, Form 2 Lead Reviewer! You are reviewing 3 collaborative group project documents (4 issues per document, 12 total).

  • Spelling Mistakes (Red Squiggly): Click the word to replace genuine misspellings with the correct technical term.
  • Trinidad & Tobago Terms (False Flags): Automated spell checkers often flag real Caribbean names (e.g. Maracas, Callaloo, Parang, Scarborough). Choose Ignore Once so you don't corrupt authentic local words!
  • Grammar Mistakes (Blue Squiggly): Click to fix subject-verb agreement or incorrect prepositions.
  • Unclear / Vague Statements (Yellow Highlight): Add a Margin Comment asking your group member for clarification, instead of guessing and silently rewriting their sentence!
  • Track Changes Button: Click the ✏️ Track Changes button in the ribbon to turn tracking ON/OFF. When correcting factual errors, Track Changes must be ON!
  • Peer Tracked Changes (Strikethrough / Underline): Click the tracked edit, then use ✅ Accept or ❌ Reject in the toolbar or inspector!
