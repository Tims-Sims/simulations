How to Play TOC X-Ray

Welcome, Form 2 Document Architect! You are organizing a 4-page group project document where Page 1 is the Table of Contents.

  • Page 1 Table of Contents & Side Widget: Both the first page and the side widget display the Table of Contents. They wait for your manual refresh to update!
  • The Problem: Some section titles on Pages 2, 3, and 4 look bold and large, but have only been given visual formatting instead of a true Heading Style.
  • X-Ray Mode: Click 🩻 X-Ray Mode in the toolbar to see the hidden structural tags (Heading 1, Heading 2, or Normal Text).
  • Repair Styles: Click any missing section title in the document, then click Heading 1 (main topic) or Heading 2 (subtopic) from the Styles Palette!
  • Manual TOC Update: Click ⚡ Generate / Update TOC to scan the document. Correctly styled headings compile into Page 1 and the side widget with automatic indentation and page numbers!
