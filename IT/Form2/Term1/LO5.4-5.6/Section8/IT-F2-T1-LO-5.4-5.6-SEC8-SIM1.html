How to Play Sprite Pen Plotter

Welcome to the Trinidad and Tobago Secondary School Scratch IT Lab! Your goal is to guide the sprite to draw target patterns across 4 missions.

  • 📦 Block Palette (Left): Click any block to add it to your script workspace in the middle.
  • 📜 Script Workspace: Arrange your blocks top-to-bottom. Use ⬆️ / ⬇️ to reorder, or ✖️ to remove a block.
  • ✏️ Pen Down vs 🖊️ Pen Up: pen down lowers the pen to draw as the sprite moves. pen up lifts the pen so the sprite can move across gaps without leaving a trail.
  • 🧭 Move & Turn: move travels forward in the current direction. turn ↻ 90 deg rotates clockwise; turn ↺ 90 deg rotates counter-clockwise.
  • ➕ Block Summing & Alternative Solutions: Multiple move blocks (like two move 50 blocks) sum together to 100 steps! Both clockwise and counter-clockwise squares, as well as alternative valid L-turn orientations, are fully accepted.
  • 🏁 Run (Green Flag): Click 🏁 Go to watch your code execute step-by-step!
