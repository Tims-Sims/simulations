🎬 The 4 Essential Pillars of a Storyboard Frame

A storyboard frame is more than a picture. It communicates everything the production crew needs to film the scene:

1. VISUAL

What the audience sees. Characters, actors, props, camera angle, and scene environment.

2. ACTION

What happens in the shot. Clear written description of physical movement, gestures, and events.

3. AUDIO

What the audience hears. Spoken dialogue, voiceover, sound effects (SFX), and ambient background audio.

4. TIMING

How long the shot lasts. Precise duration in seconds so the scene feels natural without being rushed or lagging.

How to Build a Storyboard Shot

Welcome, Video Planner! Your mission is to construct a complete storyboard frame with 4 connected components:

  • 1. Visual: Click the prop chips below the frame to place the required actor (student), prop (litter), and destination (rubbish bin).
  • 2. Action: Pick the action card from the right panel that accurately describes the script brief.
  • 3. Audio: Select relevant dialogue (“Let’s keep our school clean!”), sound effect (trash dropping), and schoolyard ambience.
  • 4. Timing: Drag the duration slider to set natural pacing (4.0s – 6.0s).
  • 5. Preview Shot: Click ▶ Preview Shot to watch your planned scene animate in real-time and discover improvements!
