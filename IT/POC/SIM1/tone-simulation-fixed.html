📘 Tone Guide

😊 Polite

  • Uses respectful words
  • Says please or thank you
  • Shows appreciation
  • Makes kind requests

😠 Rude

  • Dismissive language
  • Blames others
  • Little respect
  • Can hurt feelings

🤔 Unclear

  • Missing information
  • Short replies
  • Can mean many things

😡 Too Aggressive

  • ALL CAPS
  • Demands
  • Strong commands
  • Too intense
