How to Play IPOS Cycle Simulator

Welcome! Learn how computers process data through the 4 steps of the IPOS cycle:

  • 📥 Input: Devices used to enter data (Keyboard, Mouse).
  • ⚙️ Processing: Component that performs calculations (CPU).
  • 📤 Output: Devices that display or present processed results (Monitor, Printer, Speakers).
  • 💾 Storage: Devices that save files permanently (Hard Drive, Flash Drive).
  • 🔄 Shifting Items: You can drag and shift items between categories anytime before submitting.
  • ✅ Submit Check: Place all 8 devices into zones, then click Submit IPOS Cycle!
  • ⚡ Start Calculation: Once submitted correctly, click [⚡ Start IPOS Calculation] to watch the data flow!
