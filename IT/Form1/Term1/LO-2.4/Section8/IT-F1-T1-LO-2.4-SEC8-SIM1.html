How to Play Desk Rescue

Welcome, Cadet! Step through 12 workstation scenarios (1 scenario per page) to identify and eliminate physical computer hazards.

  • Watch the Animated SVG Room: Inspect the 2D room view to identify the hazard (spills, blocked heat vents, dust, magnets, lightning surges, or rough drops).
  • Drag the Real Tool: Click and physically drag a tool from your Rescue Inventory Toolkit directly onto the animated room scene to apply the fix.
  • Monitor Health & Temperature: Correct placements cool down the CPU temp meter and restore health. Watch out for dangerous distractors (e.g. wet sponges on dust vents) that cause short-circuits!
  • Complete All 12 Scenarios: Neutralize all 12 scenarios to earn your Master Hardware Defender certification!
