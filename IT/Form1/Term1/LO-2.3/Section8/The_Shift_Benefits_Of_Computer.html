How to Play: The Shift

Welcome to San Fernando Secondary School Cafeteria & Store! For each of the 4 scenarios, you must complete The Old Way (Manual) task first before unlocking The New Way (Computer) system:

  • Scenario 1 (Speed): Flip paper ledger pages to search for Red Solo & Cheese Paste sandwich vs POS barcode scanner.
  • Scenario 2 (Accuracy): Fix the paper subtraction error ($20.00 - $14.50 = $4.50 mistake) by typing the correct change due vs POS automated change.
  • Scenario 3 (Storage Capacity): Drag paper boxes into a filing cabinet that only fits 2 boxes (causing drawer jam & overflow) vs saving 10,000 receipts to a digital hard drive.
  • Scenario 4 (Reliability & Consistency): Stamp 20 slips manually in sets of 5 with 3-second hand rest cooldowns vs running a batch print script.
