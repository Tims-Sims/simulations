How to Play IT ICT CS Sorter

Welcome! Your goal is to sort 30 real-world tech scenarios into their correct domain bins across 5 rounds.

  • 🔐 IT (Information Technology): Infrastructure, hardware setup, network security, backups, and user accounts.
  • 📡 ICT (Information & Communication Tech): Communication tools, emailing, live streaming, social media, and digital collaboration.
  • 🧠 CS (Computer Science): Algorithms, coding logic, Scratch/Python programs, and problem-solving logic.
  • 🔄 Shifting Cards: Drag and move cards between bins anytime before submitting!
