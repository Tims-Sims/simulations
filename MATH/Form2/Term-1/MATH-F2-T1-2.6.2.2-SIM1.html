Kite Camp

Easter breeze over the Savannah, and the kite camp bench is piling up with deliveries! Sealed packs hide how many pieces are inside — loose pieces you can count. The number in front counts the equal groups, a letter stands for the hidden amount in one group, and counted loose ones join on with a plus sign. Build each delivery as a drawing, then as maths — and fly a kite for every one you match.
