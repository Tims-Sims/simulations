Buccoo Reef Treasure Vault

Sunken treasure lies on Buccoo Reef, and salvage-master Clawdia the crab needs a sharp diver. Every wreck counts its coins in a different number base: coins pack into pouches, pouches into chests — and each column is worth the base times the column to its right. Pack coins, crank up the headings, weigh glowing digits, and catch fake crates to string your pearl necklace.
