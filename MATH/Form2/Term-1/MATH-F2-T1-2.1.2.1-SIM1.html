The Number Forge

Fire up the forge! Numbers arrive as glowing metal blocks — swap them, clamp them into brackets, split them apart and hammer out the answer. The three laws of numbers are your forge moves: commutative (swap), associative (regroup), distributive (split). Blocks that break the law refuse to move. Forge all 8 pieces to become a master smith!
