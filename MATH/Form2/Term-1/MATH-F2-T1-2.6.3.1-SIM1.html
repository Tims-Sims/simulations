Dawn at the wharf, Weigh-Master! Slips are piling up in your tray. Some just list amounts — those are expressions, and they go on the price board. Some claim two sides match — those are equations, and the equals sign is the hook that hangs them on the big scale. Only a claim can be solved, so read each slip carefully and put it where it belongs.
