How to Use the Video Editing Studio

Welcome! Your task is to turn raw footage of a school table tennis tournament into an edited video:

  • Placing & Reordering Clips: Click a clip in the Media Pool to select it, then click ANY slot on the Video Track. Click two filled slots to SWAP their positions freely.
  • Clip Durations & Trimming: Raw Clip 2 is 5 seconds long (contains 2s of camera pointed at the floor + 3s of match rally in ANY slot). Click Clip 2 on the timeline and click [✂️ Trim 2s] to trim it to 3s! If left untrimmed, the needle visibly sweeps through the 2s floor section before reaching the rally!
  • Text Track Overlays: Click a Text Tool (Title, Subtitle, Credits), then click a slot on the Text Track to place it. Text does NOT auto-fill—you control exactly which slot each text item goes into!
  • Screening Video: Press ▶ Play Timeline to preview your cut with geometric needle precision and fluid animated SVGs!
