📘 Sorting guide

Use the safest available path.

✅ Keep materials useful

  • Reuse safe items your school still needs.
  • Donate safe, working surplus to an approved organization.

♻ Send materials onward

  • Recycle clean paper or equipment accepted by a certified recycler.
  • Safe Disposal batteries and damaged power items at an approved collection point.

⚠ Use your school’s collection rules and local program instructions. Acceptance can vary by community.
