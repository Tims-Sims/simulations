How to Play Workstation Ergonomics Fix

Welcome, Ergonomics Investigator! Your job is to fix 12 un-ergonomic workstation issues across 3 rounds.

  • Drag Accessories: Click and drag an accessory card from the right palette.
  • Drop onto Joints: Drop the accessory onto the matching red joint circle on the avatar (Eye/Neck, Spine/Back, Wrists/Arms, Feet/Legs).
  • Watch the Straighten Animation: Placing the correct accessory automatically straightens the body part and turns the joint indicator Green 🟢!
  • Complete All Rounds: Fix all 4 body joints on each round to advance!
