How the Energy Balance Scale Works

Welcome to Form 2 Biology Lab! Energy in food and energy output govern body mass.

  • Starting State: The scale starts perfectly balanced with 0 food selected and 0 energy output (0 kJ vs 0 kJ).
  • Energy Intake (🍔 Left Pan): Add food and drink items (measured in Kilojoules / kJ and Calories / kcal).
  • Energy Output (🏃 Right Pan): Add physical activities and adjust the Genetics BMR slider.
  • Weight Maintenance (⚖️ Level Scale): Energy Intake = Energy Output. Body mass remains constant.
  • Weight Gain / Surplus (🍔 Left Pan Low): Energy Intake > Energy Output. Excess energy stored as body fat. The food side tilts DOWN.
  • Weight Loss / Deficit (🏃 Right Pan Low): Energy Output > Energy Intake. Body uses stored energy reserves. The activity side tilts DOWN.
