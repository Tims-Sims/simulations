Nutrient Review

Know Your Nutrients

Review the seven nutrients and learn how they help your body stay healthy, grow and function properly.

💡 Activity Tip

Many foods contain more than one nutrient. In this activity, choose the nutrient that represents the food's main contribution. Some foods may also fit other nutrient groups, but the main group is the best answer.

Carbohydrates

What it does

Provides the body's main source of energy for movement, thinking and everyday activities.

How to spot it

Think starch or sweetness. Carbohydrates generally fall into these two forms: starches, which break down more slowly, and sugars, which provide quicker energy.

Proteins

What it does

Provides materials the body needs for growth and for building, repairing and maintaining tissues.

How to spot it

Think muscle, animal or plant-based sources.. Protein can come from both animal and plant sources.

Fats

What it does

Provides concentrated energy and helps insulate the body, protect organs, absorb certain vitamins, and build cell membranes.

How to spot it

Think rich or oily foods. Fats are often found in foods that feel oily, creamy, or rich.

Vitamins

What it does

Help regulate many processes in the body, including vision, immunity, wound healing, and maintaining healthy tissues.

How to spot it

Think variety and small amounts. Vitamins are needed in small quantities and are found across a wide range of foods, with different vitamins providing different benefits.

Minerals

What it does

Support important body functions and help build strong bones, teeth, and healthy blood. Calcium and iron are two important examples.

How to spot it

Think natural elements. Minerals come from the earth and are present in foods as substances the body needs in small amounts.

Water

What it does

Helps maintain hydration and transport nutrients and waste, regulate body temperature, support cells, and lubricate joints and tissues.

How to spot it

Think liquid and moisture. Water is the body's essential liquid and is also naturally present in many things we consume.

Fibre

What it does

Helps food and waste move through the digestive system by adding bulk and holding water, helping prevent constipation.

How to spot it

Think roughage and plant structure. Fibre is the part of certain foods that the body cannot digest and absorb.
