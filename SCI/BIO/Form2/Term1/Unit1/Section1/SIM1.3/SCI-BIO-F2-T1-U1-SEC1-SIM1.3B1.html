How to Play Enzyme Matcher

Welcome, Form 2 Biologist! Your goal is to match food items to their correct digestive enzyme across 3 lab rounds.

  • Carbohydrases (Rectangular Substrates): Break down complex carbohydrates (starches like potatoes, bread, rice) into Simple Sugars (glucose).
  • Proteases (Trapezoidal Substrates): Break down long protein chains (fish, chicken, egg white) into Amino Acids.
  • Lipases (Oval Substrates): Break down lipids/fats (cooking oil, butter, coconut oil) into Fatty Acids & Glycerol.
  • Interaction: Drag food cards using the blue handle 🔵 or click a food card and then click the target enzyme lock station. Alternatively, use the ♿ Text Dropdowns tab.
  • Watch the Lock: Correct matches fit into the specialized active site shapes (Rectangle, Trapezoid, Oval) and produce animated end products in the collection bin!
