Food Test Guide

Use this guide whenever you need to check a reagent, procedure, or positive result.

I

Iodine test

Tests for: Starch.
Procedure: Add iodine directly.
Positive result: Yellow-brown changes to blue-black.
What it means: Starch, a complex carbohydrate, is present.

B

Biuret test

Tests for: Protein.
Procedure: Add Biuret reagent and swirl gently.
Positive result: Blue changes to purple or lilac.
What it means: Protein is present.

H

Benedict's test

Tests for: Reducing sugar.
Procedure: Add Benedict's solution and heat safely in a hot water bath.
Positive result: Green, yellow, orange, or brick-red appears.
What it means: Reducing sugar, a simple carbohydrate, is present.
