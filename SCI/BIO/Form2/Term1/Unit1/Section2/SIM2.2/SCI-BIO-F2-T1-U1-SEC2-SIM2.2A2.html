How to Complete Graphing Exercise Data

Welcome, Biology Student! Follow these steps to graph resting, exercise, and recovery pulse rates:

  • Plot Data Points: Refer to the Sample Data Table. Click & drag each blue point 🔵 vertically on its Minute line, or use the + / - buttons below the graph to set the exact pulse rate (bpm).
  • Draw Line & Verify: Click ✍️ Draw Line & Verify Graph to check your points. When correct, the trend line connects the points, steppers are removed, and the biology questions unlock one by one!
  • One-by-One Questions: Answer each question, check your response, and click Next Question → to proceed.
  • Review & Submit: Check your final score and review feedback in the Review Memory drawer anytime!
