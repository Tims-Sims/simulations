Systole is the squeeze. Blood moves from the right ventricle to the pulmonary artery and from the left ventricle to the aorta. Diastole is the rest. The right and left ventricle fill with blood again.

SystoleAV valves close → outlet valves open → blood moves out
DiastoleOutlet valves close → AV valves open → blood moves in

Valves keep blood moving one way.
