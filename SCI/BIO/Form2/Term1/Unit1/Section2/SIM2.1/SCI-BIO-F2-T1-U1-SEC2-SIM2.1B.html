Why are the lung vessels exceptions?

In the body circuit, arteries carry oxygenated blood. Veins carry deoxygenated blood.

The lung circuit is different. A vessel is named by its direction, not by its oxygen level. Arteries carry blood away from the heart. Veins carry blood towards the heart.

You traced the transport network

  • Arteries carry blood away from the heart at high blood pressure. Their walls are thick and muscular.
  • Veins carry blood towards the heart at low blood pressure. Their walls are thinner, and valves help prevent backflow.
  • Capillaries have the lowest blood pressure and walls one cell thick. This allows exchange in the lungs and body tissues.
  • The pulmonary artery is an exception. It carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs.
  • The pulmonary vein is an exception. It carries oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart.
